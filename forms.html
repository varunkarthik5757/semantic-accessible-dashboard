Accessibility Guidelines Followed

  • Semantic Tags: Proper use of <fieldset> and <legend> for grouping related controls.
  • Labeling: Every input has an explicit associated <label> element.
  • Required Fields: Form inputs use aria-required="true" and visually indicate requirement without relying solely on color.
  • Error Identification: Form validation alerts are linked to inputs via aria-describedby and have role="alert".
  • Focus Indication: Visible focus rings for all interactive elements via :focus-visible styles.
  • Contrast Ratio: Text and background colors meet WCAG 2.1 AA requirements.
